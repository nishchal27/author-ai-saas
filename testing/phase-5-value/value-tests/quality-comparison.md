# Test V-002: Quality Comparison

## Test Information

- **Test ID**: V-002
- **Test Name**: Quality Comparison (Manual vs. App)
- **Value Dimension**: Quality Match
- **Priority**: Critical

## Objective

Compare quality of manual work vs. app-generated content:
1. Quality of manual content
2. Quality of app content
3. Quality match assessment
4. Quality improvement/degradation

## Evaluation Criteria

### Quality Match Thresholds
- **Excellent**: App quality ≥ Manual quality (or better)
- **Good**: App quality within 0.5 of manual
- **Acceptable**: App quality within 1.0 of manual
- **Poor**: App quality > 1.0 below manual

### Success Criteria
- App quality ≥ Manual quality (or very close)
- No significant quality degradation
- Publication readiness is comparable
