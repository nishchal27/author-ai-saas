# Test Q-005: Brand Voice Consistency

## Test Information

- **Test ID**: Q-005
- **Test Name**: Brand Voice Consistency
- **Quality Dimension**: Consistency Across Content
- **Priority**: Medium

---

## Objective

Test brand voice consistency across multiple content pieces:
1. Tone is consistent
2. Style is consistent
3. Language is consistent
4. Voice matches brand preferences (if set)
5. Consistency maintained across platforms

---

## Prerequisites

- User account created and logged in
- At least one product created
- Brand voice preferences set (if available)
- Multiple content pieces generated (same product, different activities)

---

## Test Steps

### Step 1: Set Brand Voice (If Available)
1. Go to Settings
2. Set brand voice preferences:
   - Tone (professional, casual, technical, etc.)
   - Style preferences
   - Language preferences
3. Save preferences

**Expected Result**: Brand voice preferences saved

---

### Step 2: Generate Multiple Content Pieces
1. Create multiple activities for same product
2. Extract insights for each
3. Generate content (same type, same platform) for each insight
4. Generate at least 5-10 content pieces

**Expected Result**: Multiple content pieces generated

---

### Step 3: Evaluate Tone Consistency
1. Review all content pieces
2. Assess tone consistency:
   - [ ] Tone is similar across pieces
   - [ ] Tone matches brand preferences (if set)
   - [ ] Tone matches product strategy
   - [ ] No major tone shifts

**Expected Result**: Tone is consistent across content

---

### Step 4: Evaluate Style Consistency
1. Review all content pieces
2. Assess style consistency:
   - [ ] Writing style is similar
   - [ ] Structure is consistent
   - [ ] Formatting is consistent
   - [ ] Voice is consistent

**Expected Result**: Style is consistent across content

---

### Step 5: Evaluate Language Consistency
1. Review all content pieces
2. Assess language consistency:
   - [ ] Vocabulary is consistent
   - [ ] Terminology is consistent
   - [ ] Language level is consistent
   - [ ] No conflicting language

**Expected Result**: Language is consistent across content

---

### Step 6: Compare with Brand Preferences
1. If brand voice is set, compare content with preferences
2. Assess:
   - [ ] Content matches preferences
   - [ ] Preferences are applied consistently
   - [ ] No deviations from preferences

**Expected Result**: Content matches brand preferences

---

### Step 7: Test Consistency Across Platforms
1. Generate content for same insight across platforms
2. Assess:
   - [ ] Voice is consistent (adapted for platform)
   - [ ] Tone is consistent (adapted for platform)
   - [ ] Brand identity maintained

**Expected Result**: Consistency maintained while adapting to platforms

---

### Step 8: Test Consistency Over Time
1. Generate content over multiple sessions
2. Assess:
   - [ ] Consistency maintained over time
   - [ ] No drift in voice
   - [ ] Brand identity stable

**Expected Result**: Consistency maintained over time

---

## Evaluation Criteria

### Consistency Scoring (1-5)

For content pieces, assess:
- **Tone Consistency**: [1-5]
  - 5: Highly consistent tone
  - 1: Inconsistent tone

- **Style Consistency**: [1-5]
  - 5: Highly consistent style
  - 1: Inconsistent style

- **Language Consistency**: [1-5]
  - 5: Highly consistent language
  - 1: Inconsistent language

- **Brand Match**: [1-5] (if brand voice set)
  - 5: Perfect match
  - 1: Doesn't match

### Success Criteria
- Average consistency score ≥ 4.0/5.0
- No major inconsistencies
- Brand voice maintained (if set)
- Consistency across platforms and time
