# Content Quality Rubric

## Overview

This rubric provides a standardized scoring system for evaluating content quality across all dimensions.

---

## Scoring Scale

- **5**: Excellent - Exceeds expectations
- **4**: Good - Meets expectations
- **3**: Acceptable - Meets minimum requirements
- **2**: Poor - Below expectations
- **1**: Very Poor - Fails to meet requirements

---

## Quality Dimensions

### 1. Relevance (1-5)

**Definition**: Content is relevant to the activity, product, and target audience.

**Scoring Criteria**:
- **5**: Highly relevant, directly addresses activity and product, perfectly matches audience
- **4**: Mostly relevant, minor gaps in relevance
- **3**: Somewhat relevant, some gaps in relevance
- **2**: Partially relevant, significant gaps
- **1**: Not relevant, doesn't address activity or product

**Questions to Ask**:
- Is content directly related to the activity?
- Is content relevant to the product?
- Is content appropriate for the target audience?
- Does content address the core value proposition?

---

### 2. Clarity (1-5)

**Definition**: Content is clear, easy to understand, and well-structured.

**Scoring Criteria**:
- **5**: Crystal clear, no ambiguity, excellent structure
- **4**: Mostly clear, minor ambiguities, good structure
- **3**: Generally clear, some confusion possible, acceptable structure
- **2**: Somewhat unclear, needs clarification, poor structure
- **1**: Unclear, confusing, no structure

**Questions to Ask**:
- Is content easy to understand?
- Is language appropriate for audience?
- Is structure logical?
- Are there any confusing statements?

---

### 3. Value (1-5)

**Definition**: Content provides value to the audience (informative, actionable, or educational).

**Scoring Criteria**:
- **5**: High value, actionable, informative, solves problems
- **4**: Good value, mostly useful, somewhat actionable
- **3**: Some value, partially useful, limited actionability
- **2**: Limited value, mostly promotional, not actionable
- **1**: No value, purely promotional, not useful

**Questions to Ask**:
- Does content provide value to audience?
- Is content actionable or informative?
- Does content solve a problem or answer a question?
- Is content more than just promotional?

---

### 4. Authenticity (1-5)

**Definition**: Content sounds like a real founder, not a marketer or AI.

**Scoring Criteria**:
- **5**: Highly authentic, sounds like real founder, honest and credible
- **4**: Mostly authentic, minor marketing language
- **3**: Somewhat authentic, some marketing speak
- **2**: Partially authentic, too much marketing language
- **1**: Not authentic, pure marketing speak, sounds like AI

**Questions to Ask**:
- Does content sound like a real founder?
- Is content honest and credible?
- Does content avoid marketing hype?
- Does content feel authentic, not templated?

---

### 5. Strategy Alignment (1-5)

**Definition**: Content follows product strategy recommendations.

**Scoring Criteria**:
- **5**: Perfectly aligned, follows all strategy recommendations
- **4**: Mostly aligned, follows most recommendations
- **3**: Somewhat aligned, follows some recommendations
- **2**: Partially aligned, doesn't follow key recommendations
- **1**: Not aligned, violates strategy recommendations

**Questions to Ask**:
- Does content use recommended platforms?
- Does content follow tone recommendations?
- Does content use recommended proof types?
- Does content avoid anti-patterns?

---

### 6. Platform Fit (1-5)

**Definition**: Content is optimized for the target platform.

**Scoring Criteria**:
- **5**: Perfectly optimized, follows all platform best practices
- **4**: Mostly optimized, follows most best practices
- **3**: Somewhat optimized, follows some best practices
- **2**: Partially optimized, doesn't follow key best practices
- **1**: Not optimized, wrong format for platform

**Questions to Ask**:
- Is content format appropriate for platform?
- Is content length appropriate?
- Does content follow platform best practices?
- Is content optimized for platform audience?

---

## Overall Quality Score

Calculate average of all dimensions:
- **Overall Score** = (Relevance + Clarity + Value + Authenticity + Strategy Alignment + Platform Fit) / 6

### Quality Thresholds
- **Excellent**: ≥ 4.5/5.0
- **Good**: ≥ 4.0/5.0
- **Acceptable**: ≥ 3.5/5.0
- **Needs Improvement**: < 3.5/5.0

---

## Publication Readiness

Assess if content can be published with minimal edits:
- **Publication-Ready**: No edits needed (5/5)
- **Minor Edits**: Small tweaks needed (4/5)
- **Some Edits**: Moderate edits needed (3/5)
- **Significant Edits**: Major edits needed (2/5)
- **Major Rewrite**: Complete rewrite needed (1/5)

---

## Usage Notes

- Use this rubric consistently across all content evaluations
- Score each dimension independently
- Calculate overall score as average
- Document specific examples for each score
- Compare scores across different content pieces
- Use scores to identify improvement areas
