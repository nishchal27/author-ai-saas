2️⃣ Product → Post Decision Engine (STRONG)
What it is

A tool that answers:

“Based on what I built this week, what should I post and why?”

Input:

Short description of:

What changed

What shipped

What problem was solved

Output:

2–3 recommended post angles

Platform recommendation (X / LinkedIn / both)

What NOT to post (anti-pattern)

Reasoning in plain language

Example

“You fixed onboarding drop-off”

Output:

❌ Don’t post a feature list

✅ Post a before/after onboarding insight

✅ Post a short dev lesson on what failed

Platform: X > LinkedIn

Reason: early-stage dev SaaS, credibility > polish

Why this is powerful

This is decision relief

Devs struggle here daily

No generic AI tool does this well

Demand

🔥 High

🔥 Reusable weekly

🔥 Deeply aligned with AuthorAI’s core

Verdict

✅ Extremely strong sub-MVP
✅ High perceived intelligence
✅ Hard to replace with ChatGPT



4️⃣ Weekly “Build → Content Map” Generator (VERY STRONG)

A weekly ritual tool.

Input:

Bullet list of what you worked on this week

Output:

3–5 content opportunities

Each mapped to:

Insight

Post type

Platform

Why it works

This feels like:

“I worked. Now tell me what content I earned.”

Why this is powerful

Feels fair, not grindy

Demand

🔥 High for indie founders

🔁 Weekly habit

🧠 Very aligned with build-in-public

✅ One of the best fits for AuthorAI
